# Steamed Peas

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 cups Fresh or Frozen Peas  
- [ ] 1–2 tbsp Water  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] 1 tsp Butter or Olive Oil (optional)  
- [ ] Optional: Fresh herbs (mint, parsley, or thyme)  

## Instructions

- Bring 1–2 inches of water to a boil in a saucepan or pot fitted with a steaming basket  
- Add peas to the steaming basket  
- Cover and steam for 3–5 minutes for fresh peas, 2–4 minutes for frozen, until tender and bright green  
- Remove from heat, season with salt and black pepper  
- Optional: Toss with butter or olive oil and sprinkle with fresh herbs  
- Serve immediately  

## Notes

- Do not overcook — peas should be tender but slightly crisp  
- Steaming preserves nutrients, color, and natural sweetness  
- Can be added to salads, grain bowls, or served as a simple side

# Pan-Fried Carrots

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

[ ] 1 lb Carrots, peeled and sliced into sticks or rounds  
[ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil or Butter  
[ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced (optional)  
[ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
[ ] 1 tsp Fresh or Dried Herbs (thyme, parsley, or rosemary)  
[ ] 1 tsp Honey or Maple Syrup (optional, for a touch of sweetness)  

## Instructions

- Heat olive oil or butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat  
- Add carrots and sauté 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they start to brown  
- Add garlic and herbs; cook 1–2 minutes until fragrant  
- Optional: Drizzle with honey or maple syrup and toss to coat  
- Season with salt and black pepper to taste  
- Serve hot as a side dish  

## Notes

- Slice carrots evenly for uniform cooking  
- Can add a squeeze of lemon juice for brightness  
- For extra caramelization, cover pan briefly and then uncover to brown  
- Pairs well with roasted meats, chicken, or fish

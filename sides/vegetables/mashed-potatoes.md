# Mashed Potatoes

Servings: 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 lbs Potatoes (Yukon Gold, Russet, or a mix), peeled and cut into chunks  
- [ ] 4 tbsp Butter  
- [ ] 1/2–3/4 cup Milk or Cream (warm)  
- [ ] Salt, to taste  
- [ ] Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] Optional: Garlic (2–3 cloves, roasted or boiled with potatoes)  
- [ ] Optional: Chives or parsley, chopped, for garnish  

## Instructions

- Place potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water  
- Add a pinch of salt and bring to a boil over medium-high heat  
- Reduce heat and simmer 15–20 minutes, or until potatoes are fork-tender  
- Drain potatoes and return to the pot  
- Mash potatoes using a masher or ricer  
- Stir in butter and warm milk or cream until smooth and creamy  
- Season with salt and black pepper to taste  
- Optional: Fold in roasted garlic for extra flavor  
- Garnish with chopped chives or parsley before serving  

## Notes

- For extra creamy potatoes, use Yukon Golds or a mix with Russets  
- Warm the milk or cream before adding to keep potatoes fluffy  
- Do not overmix to avoid gummy texture  
- Mashed potatoes pair well with roasted meats, gravy, or vegetables

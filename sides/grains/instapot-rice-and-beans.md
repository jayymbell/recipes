# Instant Pot Rice and Beans

**Time Estimate:** Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Natural Release: 10 min | Total: 25 min  
**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 cup Long-Grain White Rice (or brown rice)  
- [ ] 1 can (15 oz) Black Beans or Pinto Beans, drained & rinsed  
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups Water or Chicken/Vegetable Broth (use 1 1/2 cups for brown rice)  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1 small Onion, diced  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Cumin  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Paprika  
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Chili Powder (optional)  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Fresh Cilantro, chopped (optional)  
- [ ] 1 Lime, cut into wedges (optional)  

## Instructions

- Turn Instant Pot to sauté mode and heat olive oil  
- Add onion and sauté 2–3 minutes until softened  
- Add garlic, cumin, paprika, and chili powder; cook 30 seconds until fragrant  
- Add rice and stir to coat in spices  
- Pour in water or broth and stir to combine  
- Seal Instant Pot lid and set to **Pressure Cook / Manual**:  
  - White rice: 4–6 minutes high pressure  
  - Brown rice: 20–22 minutes high pressure  
- Allow **Natural Pressure Release** for 10 minutes, then carefully release remaining pressure  
- Stir in drained beans and season with salt and pepper  
- Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with lime wedges if desired  

## Notes

- For a creamier texture, add beans before cooking and increase liquid slightly  
- Can add corn, bell peppers, or diced tomatoes for a Southwest-style version  
- Leftovers store well and can be reheated in the Instant Pot or microwave  
- Brown rice requires more water and longer cooking time than white rice

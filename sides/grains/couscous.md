# Couscous

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 cup Couscous  
- [ ] 1 1/4 cup Water or Broth (chicken or vegetable)  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Olive Oil or Butter  
- [ ] Salt, to taste  
- [ ] Optional: Fresh herbs (parsley, cilantro, or mint), lemon zest, or toasted nuts  

## Instructions

- Bring water or broth to a boil in a small saucepan  
- Add olive oil or butter and a pinch of salt  
- Remove from heat and stir in couscous  
- Cover tightly and let sit 5 minutes  
- Fluff with a fork before serving  
- Optional: Stir in fresh herbs, lemon zest, or toasted nuts for extra flavor  

## Notes

- Using broth instead of water adds more depth of flavor  
- Couscous cooks very quickly — do not over-steam or it becomes mushy  
- Can serve hot as a side, or cold as a base for salads  
- Pairs well with roasted vegetables, chicken, fish, or Mediterranean dishes

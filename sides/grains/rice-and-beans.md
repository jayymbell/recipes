# Rice and Beans

**Time Estimate:** Prep: 5 min | Cook: 25–30 min | Total: 30–35 min  
**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 cup Long-Grain White Rice (or brown rice)  
- [ ] 2 cups Water or Chicken/Vegetable Broth  
- [ ] 1 can (15 oz) Black Beans or Pinto Beans, drained & rinsed  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1 small Onion, diced  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Cumin  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Paprika  
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Chili Powder (optional)  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Fresh Cilantro, chopped (optional)  
- [ ] 1 Lime, cut into wedges (optional)  

## Instructions

- Heat olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat  
- Add onion and sauté 3–4 minutes until softened  
- Add garlic, cumin, paprika, and chili powder; cook 30 seconds until fragrant  
- Stir in rice and toast for 1–2 minutes  
- Add water or broth, bring to a boil  
- Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer 15–20 minutes (or according to rice package instructions) until liquid is absorbed  
- Remove from heat and let sit 5 minutes, covered  
- Stir in drained beans and gently mix to combine  
- Season with salt and pepper to taste  
- Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with lime wedges if desired  

## Notes

- For extra flavor, sauté rice with a small amount of tomato paste or roasted peppers  
- Can substitute brown rice; increase cooking time and liquid as needed  
- Add cooked sausage, chicken, or shrimp to make it a complete meal  
- Leftovers reheat well and can be used in burritos or bowls

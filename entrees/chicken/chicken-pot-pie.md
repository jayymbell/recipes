# Chicken Pot Pie

Servings: 4–6

## Ingredients

[ ] 1 lb Chicken Breast or Thighs, cooked & diced  
[ ] 1 Refrigerated Pie Crust (or 2 for top & bottom)  
[ ] 1 c Chicken Broth  
[ ] 1 c Milk or Heavy Cream  
[ ] 2 tbsp Butter  
[ ] 2 tbsp All-Purpose Flour  
[ ] 1 medium Onion, diced  
[ ] 2 Carrots, diced  
[ ] 2 Celery stalks, diced  
[ ] 1 c Frozen Peas  
[ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced  
[ ] 1 tsp Dried Thyme  
[ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C)  
- Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat  
- Add onion, carrots, and celery; cook 6–8 minutes until tender  
- Stir in garlic and thyme; cook 30 seconds  
- Sprinkle flour over vegetables and stir 1 minute  
- Slowly whisk in chicken broth and milk until thickened  
- Stir in chicken and peas; season with salt and pepper  
- Pour filling into pie dish and top with crust  
- Cut slits in crust to vent  
- Bake 30–35 minutes until golden and bubbly  
- Cool 10 minutes before serving  

## Notes

- Use rotisserie chicken to save time  
- Brush crust with egg wash for extra golden color  
- Let filling cool slightly before baking to avoid soggy crust

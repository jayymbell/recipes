# Chicken Stir Fry

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Chicken Breast or Thighs, thinly sliced  
- [ ] 2–3 cups Mixed Vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, snap peas, etc.)  
- [ ] 3 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Ginger, minced  
- [ ] 3 tbsp Soy Sauce  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Oyster Sauce (optional)  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Sesame Oil  
- [ ] 1–2 tbsp Vegetable Oil (for cooking)  
- [ ] 1 tsp Cornstarch (optional, for thickening)  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Water (if using cornstarch)  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] Optional: Green onions, sesame seeds for garnish  

## Instructions

- Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat  
- Add chicken and season lightly with salt and pepper; cook 4–5 minutes until browned and cooked through  
- Remove chicken from pan and set aside  
- Add garlic and ginger to the pan; sauté 30 seconds until fragrant  
- Add vegetables and stir-fry 3–5 minutes until crisp-tender  
- Return chicken to the pan  
- Stir in soy sauce, oyster sauce (if using), and sesame oil  
- Optional: Mix cornstarch with water and add to pan to thicken sauce; cook 1–2 minutes  
- Taste and adjust seasoning  
- Serve hot over rice or noodles, garnished with green onions or sesame seeds  

## Notes

- Use whatever vegetables you have on hand  
- For a spicier version, add chili flakes or sriracha  
- Chicken thighs stay juicier than breasts  
- High heat and quick cooking keep vegetables crisp and colorful

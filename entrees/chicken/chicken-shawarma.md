# Chicken Shawarma

**Time Estimate:** Prep & Marinate: 30 min–12 hrs | Cook: 50–60 min | Broil: 5 min  
**Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Marinade

- [ ] 2–2.5 lbs Boneless, Skinless Chicken Thighs  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Plain Greek Yogurt  
- [ ] 3 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Lemon Juice  
- [ ] 2 tsp Ground Cumin  
- [ ] 2 tsp Paprika  
- [ ] 1 tsp Ground Coriander  
- [ ] 1 tsp Ground Turmeric  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Ground Cinnamon  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Ground Allspice  
- [ ] 1 tsp Salt  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Black Pepper  

### For Stacking

- [ ] 1 large Onion, thickly sliced (base)  
- [ ] Wooden or Metal Skewer (oven-safe)  

### For Serving

- [ ] Pita or Flatbread  
- [ ] Tzatziki or Garlic Sauce (Toum)  
- [ ] Tomato, sliced  
- [ ] Cucumber, sliced  
- [ ] Shredded Lettuce  
- [ ] Pickles or Turnip Pickles (optional)  

## Instructions

### Marinate

- In a large bowl, mix olive oil, yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, and all spices  
- Add chicken and coat thoroughly  
- Cover and marinate at least 30 minutes (overnight strongly recommended)  

### Stack the Shawarma

- Preheat oven to **425°F (220°C)**  
- Place onion slices flat-side down in the center of a foil-lined baking dish  
- Insert skewer vertically through the onion to create a stable base  
- Stack marinated chicken thighs tightly onto the skewer, largest pieces on bottom, forming a cone shape  

### Roast

- Place dish on the lower-middle rack  
- Roast uncovered for 45–55 minutes until internal temp reaches 165°F (74°C)  
- Rotate pan halfway for even browning  

### Broil & Slice

- Switch oven to **broil (high)** for 3–5 minutes to crisp the outside  
- Let rest 10 minutes  
- Slice thinly from the outside down, like traditional shawarma  

### Serve

- Serve sliced chicken in pita or bowls with sauces and toppings  

## Notes

- Thighs are essential — breasts dry out when stacked  
- Packing chicken tightly helps mimic vertical rotisserie cooking  
- Save the drippings and spoon them over sliced meat for extra flavor  
- Can slice, then return meat to oven briefly to re-crisp

# Oven-Roasted Chicken

Servings: 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 whole Chicken (3–4 lbs), cleaned and patted dry  
- [ ] 2–3 tbsp Olive Oil or melted Butter  
- [ ] 1 tsp Salt  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Black Pepper  
- [ ] 1 tsp Paprika (optional, for color)  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Herbs (thyme, rosemary, or oregano)  
- [ ] 1 Lemon, halved  
- [ ] 4 cloves Garlic, smashed  
- [ ] Optional: Vegetables for roasting (carrots, potatoes, onions)  

### For the Wet Brine

- [ ] 4 cups Water  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Salt  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Sugar (optional, adds slight sweetness)  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, smashed  
- [ ] 1–2 sprigs Fresh Herbs (thyme, rosemary, or bay leaf)  

## Instructions

### Wet Brine

- In a saucepan, dissolve salt and sugar in water over medium heat; cool completely  
- Add garlic and herbs to the cooled brine  
- Submerge chicken in the brine in a large container or resealable bag  
- Refrigerate for 4–6 hours (or overnight for extra flavor)  
- Remove chicken from brine, rinse thoroughly, and pat dry  

### Roasting

- Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)  
- Rub the chicken all over with olive oil or butter  
- Season generously with pepper, paprika, and dried herbs (skip salt since brine adds it)  
- Stuff the cavity with lemon halves and smashed garlic  
- Optional: Arrange vegetables around the chicken in the roasting pan  
- Roast for 1–1.25 hours, or until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the thigh  
- Let the chicken rest 10–15 minutes before carving  
- Serve hot with roasted vegetables or your favorite sides  

## Notes

- Wet brining ensures the chicken stays juicy and flavorful  
- Trussing helps cook the bird evenly and keeps breast meat moist  
- Basting occasionally with pan juices adds extra flavor  
- For crispier skin, increase oven temperature to 450°F (232°C) for the last 10–15 minutes  
- Leftover chicken works well in salads, sandwiches, or soups

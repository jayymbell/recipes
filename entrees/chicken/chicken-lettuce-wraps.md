# PF Chang’s Chicken Lettuce Wraps

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Ground Chicken (or turkey)  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Vegetable Oil  
- [ ] 1 medium Onion, finely diced  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger, minced  
- [ ] 1 can Water Chestnuts, drained and chopped  
- [ ] 2 green Onions, sliced  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Soy Sauce  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Hoisin Sauce  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar  
- [ ] 1 tsp Sesame Oil  
- [ ] 1 tsp Sriracha or Chili Garlic Sauce (optional, for heat)  
- [ ] 1 head Butter or Romaine Lettuce, leaves separated and washed  
- [ ] Optional toppings: Chopped peanuts, shredded carrots, sesame seeds  

## Instructions

- Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat  
- Add onion, garlic, and ginger; sauté 2–3 minutes until fragrant  
- Add ground chicken and cook until no longer pink, breaking it up with a spoon  
- Stir in chopped water chestnuts and green onions  
- In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, hoisin sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and sriracha  
- Pour sauce over chicken mixture and cook 2–3 minutes until heated through  
- Remove from heat  
- Serve warm chicken mixture in lettuce leaves  
- Top with optional toppings like peanuts, carrots, or sesame seeds  

## Notes

- Butter lettuce works best for wraps because leaves are sturdy but tender  
- You can prep filling ahead of time and reheat before serving  
- For a lower-sodium version, use reduced-sodium soy sauce  
- Can also add shredded cabbage or bell peppers for extra crunch

# Smoked BBQ Pork Sandwiches

**Time Estimate:** Prep: 1 hr (including brine prep) | Brine: 4–6 hrs (or overnight) | Smoke: 6–8 hrs | Rest & Assemble: 30–60 min  
**Servings:** 6–8

## Ingredients

### Wet Brine

- [ ] 4 cups Water  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Salt  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Brown Sugar  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, smashed  
- [ ] 1–2 sprigs Fresh Thyme or Rosemary (optional)  
- [ ] 1 tsp Black Peppercorns  

### Pork & Rub

- [ ] 4–5 lbs Pork Shoulder (Boston Butt)  
- [ ] 2–3 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 2–3 tbsp Brown Sugar  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Paprika  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Salt  
- [ ] 1 tsp Black Pepper  
- [ ] 1 tsp Garlic Powder  
- [ ] 1 tsp Onion Powder  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper (optional)  

### Smoking & Serving

- [ ] 1 cup Apple Juice or Apple Cider (for spritzing)  
- [ ] 1–2 cups BBQ Sauce (for serving)  
- [ ] Hamburger Buns  
- [ ] Optional toppings: Coleslaw, pickles, or sliced onions  

## Instructions

### Wet Brine

- In a pot, dissolve salt and brown sugar in water; cool completely  
- Add smashed garlic, herbs, and peppercorns  
- Submerge pork shoulder in brine in a large container or resealable bag  
- Refrigerate 4–6 hours (or overnight for maximum flavor)  
- Remove pork from brine, rinse well, and pat dry  

### Prepare the Pork

- Rub pork with olive oil  
- Mix brown sugar, paprika, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and cayenne  
- Rub spice mixture all over the pork, pressing to adhere  

### Smoke the Pork

- Preheat smoker to 225–250°F (107–121°C)  
- Place pork on smoker grates, fat side up  
- Smoke 6–8 hours, spritzing with apple juice or cider every 1–2 hours, until internal temperature reaches 195–205°F (90–96°C)  

### Finish & Serve

- Remove pork from smoker, wrap in foil, and rest 30–60 minutes  
- Shred pork using forks  
- Toss shredded pork with BBQ sauce or serve sauce on the side  
- Serve on hamburger buns with optional toppings  

## Notes

- Wet brining keeps pork incredibly juicy and flavorful  
- Use apple, hickory, or cherry wood for the best smoky flavor  
- Can make ahead and refrigerate; reheat gently with sauce before serving  
- Leftovers are perfect for tacos, nachos, or salads

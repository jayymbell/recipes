# Stuffed Peppers

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 4 Large Bell Peppers, tops cut off and seeds removed  
- [ ] 1 lb Italian Sausage, removed from casing  
- [ ] 1 cup Cooked Rice (white, brown, or wild)  
- [ ] 1 small Onion, diced  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 2 cups Fresh Spinach, chopped  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Oregano  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Basil  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] ½ cup Shredded Mozzarella or Parmesan Cheese  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil  

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)  
- Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat  
- Add onion and garlic; sauté 3–4 minutes until softened  
- Add Italian sausage and cook until browned, breaking up large pieces  
- Stir in cooked rice, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper  
- Add chopped spinach and cook 2–3 minutes until wilted  
- Remove skillet from heat and let filling cool slightly  
- Stuff each bell pepper with the sausage and spinach mixture  
- Place peppers upright in a baking dish  
- Sprinkle tops with shredded cheese  
- Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes  
- Remove foil and bake an additional 10–15 minutes until cheese is golden and peppers are tender  

## Notes

- Any bell pepper color works  
- Fresh spinach adds moisture; if using frozen, thaw and squeeze excess water  
- Leftover filling can be used for stuffed mushrooms, baked pasta, or omelets

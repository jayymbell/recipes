# 30-Minute Pizza

Servings: 2–4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 package Store-Bought Pizza Dough (16 oz or similar)  
- [ ] 1/2 cup Tomato Sauce or Pizza Sauce  
- [ ] 1 1/2–2 cups Shredded Mozzarella Cheese  
- [ ] 1 tsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Oregano or Italian Seasoning  
- [ ] Optional Toppings: Pepperoni, cooked sausage, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, olives, fresh basil  

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C); if using a pizza stone, place it in oven to heat  
- Roll or stretch dough to desired thickness on a lightly floured surface  
- Transfer dough to a greased baking sheet or parchment paper  
- Brush edges lightly with olive oil  
- Spread tomato sauce evenly over dough, leaving a 1-inch border for the crust  
- Sprinkle cheese over sauce and add desired toppings  
- Sprinkle oregano or Italian seasoning over the top  
- Bake 12–15 minutes, or until crust is golden and cheese is melted and bubbly  
- Remove from oven, let cool 2–3 minutes, slice, and serve  

## Notes

- Pre-cook any raw toppings (like sausage or onions) before adding to pizza  
- For extra crispiness, bake on a preheated pizza stone or cast-iron skillet  
- Use fresh basil or arugula after baking for added flavor  
- Can substitute tomato sauce with pesto, BBQ sauce, or white sauce for variety

# Beef Stroganoff

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Beef Sirloin or Tenderloin, thinly sliced into strips  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil or Butter  
- [ ] 1 medium Onion, diced  
- [ ] 2 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 8 oz Mushrooms, sliced  
- [ ] 1 tsp Paprika  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] 1 cup Beef Broth  
- [ ] 1 tsp Worcestershire Sauce  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard  
- [ ] 1/2 cup Sour Cream  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Flour (optional, for thicker sauce)  
- [ ] Optional: Fresh Parsley, chopped, for garnish  
- [ ] Cooked Egg Noodles, Rice, or Mashed Potatoes for serving  

## Instructions

- Heat olive oil or butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat  
- Add beef strips and cook quickly until browned on all sides; remove and set aside  
- In the same skillet, sauté onions and garlic until softened, about 3–4 minutes  
- Add mushrooms and cook until they release moisture and begin to brown  
- Sprinkle in paprika, salt, and black pepper; stir to combine  
- Optional: Sprinkle flour over mushrooms and stir for 1–2 minutes to thicken  
- Pour in beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, and Dijon mustard; simmer 3–5 minutes until sauce thickens slightly  
- Return beef to the skillet and simmer 2–3 minutes to heat through  
- Remove from heat and stir in sour cream until smooth (do not boil after adding sour cream)  
- Serve over cooked egg noodles, rice, or mashed potatoes  
- Garnish with chopped parsley if desired  

## Notes

- For extra tenderness, choose a quick-cooking cut like sirloin or tenderloin  
- Can substitute Greek yogurt for sour cream for a lighter version  
- Adjust thickness of sauce by adding more broth or reducing simmer time  
- Mushrooms can be varied (cremini, button, or shiitake) for depth of flavor

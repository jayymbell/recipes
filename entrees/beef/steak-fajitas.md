# Steak Fajitas

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Flank Steak, thinly sliced  
- [ ] 1 Red Bell Pepper, sliced  
- [ ] 1 Green Bell Pepper, sliced  
- [ ] 1 Yellow Bell Pepper, sliced  
- [ ] 1 Large Onion, sliced  
- [ ] 2–3 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1 tsp Chili Powder  
- [ ] 1 tsp Ground Cumin  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Paprika  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] 8–10 Flour or Corn Tortillas  
- [ ] Optional toppings: Sour cream, guacamole, salsa, shredded cheese, lime wedges, chopped cilantro  

## Instructions

- In a bowl, toss sliced steak with chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper  
- Heat 1–2 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat  
- Add meat and cook until browned and cooked through, 5–7 minutes for chicken, 3–5 minutes for steak  
- Remove meat and set aside  
- In the same skillet, add remaining olive oil, onions, and bell peppers  
- Sauté 5–7 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp  
- Return meat to the skillet and toss everything together  
- Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or oven  
- Serve meat and vegetables on tortillas with desired toppings  

## Notes

- Marinate meat for 30 minutes in lime juice, olive oil, and fajita spices for extra flavor  
- Use a cast-iron skillet for the best sear and smoky flavor  
- Can substitute steak or chicken with shrimp or tofu  
- Slice vegetables thinly for even cooking

# Southwest Steak Bowl

**Time Estimate:** Prep: 15 min | Cook: 15–20 min | Total: 30–35 min  
**Servings:** 2–4

## Ingredients

### Steak & Marinade

- [ ] 1 lb Steak (flank, skirt, or sirloin)  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1 tsp Chili Powder  
- [ ] 1 tsp Cumin  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Paprika  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  

### Rice & Beans

- [ ] 2 cups Cooked Rice (white, brown, or cilantro-lime rice)  
- [ ] 1 can Black Beans, drained & rinsed  
- [ ] 1 cup Corn (fresh, canned, or frozen)  

### Veggies & Toppings

- [ ] 1 Red Bell Pepper, sliced  
- [ ] 1 small Red Onion, sliced  
- [ ] 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes, halved  
- [ ] 1 Avocado, sliced  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Fresh Cilantro, chopped  
- [ ] Optional: Lime wedges, shredded cheese, sour cream, or salsa  

## Instructions

### Prepare the Steak

- In a small bowl, mix olive oil, chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper  
- Rub mixture over steak and let sit 10–15 minutes (or marinate longer if desired)  
- Heat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat  
- Cook steak 3–5 minutes per side (depending on thickness) until desired doneness  
- Let rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain  

### Cook Veggies

- In the same skillet, sauté bell pepper and onion 3–4 minutes until slightly softened  
- Add corn and black beans, cook 2–3 minutes to heat through  

### Assemble the Bowl

- Divide rice among bowls  
- Top with sautéed veggies, black beans, corn, and sliced steak  
- Add cherry tomatoes, avocado, and optional toppings  
- Garnish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime  

## Notes

- Use pre-cooked or leftover rice for faster prep  
- Steak can be replaced with grilled chicken or shrimp for variation  
- Can make ahead: prep veggies and rice; cook steak just before serving  
- Add hot sauce or chipotle mayo for extra Southwest flavor

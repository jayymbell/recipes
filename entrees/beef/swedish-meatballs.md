# Swedish Meatballs

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- [ ] 1 lb Ground Beef (or half beef, half pork)  
- [ ] 1/2 cup Breadcrumbs  
- [ ] 1/4 cup Milk  
- [ ] 1 small Onion, finely diced  
- [ ] 1 Egg  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Salt  
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Black Pepper  
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Ground Nutmeg (optional)  
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Ground Allspice (optional)  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Butter or Oil (for frying)  

### Sauce

- [ ] 2 tbsp Butter  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Flour  
- [ ] 2 cups Beef Broth  
- [ ] 1/2 cup Heavy Cream  
- [ ] 1 tsp Worcestershire Sauce  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  

## Instructions

### Meatballs

- In a bowl, soak breadcrumbs in milk for a few minutes  
- Add ground meat, diced onion, egg, salt, pepper, nutmeg, and allspice; mix until combined  
- Form mixture into small meatballs, about 1–1.5 inches in diameter  
- Heat butter or oil in a large skillet over medium heat  
- Cook meatballs in batches until browned on all sides and cooked through, about 6–8 minutes  
- Remove meatballs and set aside  

### Sauce

- In the same skillet, melt butter over medium heat  
- Whisk in flour and cook 1–2 minutes to make a roux  
- Gradually whisk in beef broth and bring to a simmer  
- Stir in heavy cream and Worcestershire sauce  
- Season with salt and pepper  
- Return meatballs to the sauce and simmer 5–10 minutes until heated through and sauce thickens  

### Serve

- Serve meatballs with sauce over egg noodles, mashed potatoes, or rice  
- Optional: garnish with chopped parsley  

## Notes

- For extra flavor, sauté onions until golden before mixing into meatballs  
- Can make ahead: cook meatballs, then store in sauce in fridge for up to 2 days  
- Swedish meatballs are traditionally small; keep them uniform in size for even cooking  
- Nutmeg and allspice are optional but give that classic Scandinavian flavor

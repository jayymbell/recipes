# Beef Pot Roast

Servings: 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 3–4 lb Chuck Roast  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 3 c Beef Broth  
- [ ] 1 c Dry Red Wine  
- [ ] 1 medium Onion, sliced  
- [ ] 3 Carrots, cut into chunks  
- [ ] 3–4 Yukon Gold Potatoes, quartered  
- [ ] 3 Celery stalks, cut into chunks  
- [ ] 3 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Thyme  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Rosemary  
- [ ] 1 Bay Leaf  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C)  
- Season chuck roast generously with salt and pepper  
- Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat  
- Sear roast on all sides until deeply browned  
- Remove roast and add onion, carrots, celery, and garlic  
- Deglaze pot with red wine, scraping up browned bits  
- Simmer wine 2–3 minutes to cook off alcohol  
- Add beef broth, thyme, rosemary, and bay leaf  
- Return roast to pot and bring to a simmer  
- Cover and braise in oven for 3–3½ hours until fork-tender  
- Add potatoes during last 60–90 minutes of cooking  
- Remove bay leaf, shred or slice roast, and serve  

## Notes

- Use a dry red wine like Cabernet Sauvignon or Merlot  
- Low and slow yields the most tender roast  
- Thicken gravy with a cornstarch slurry if desired

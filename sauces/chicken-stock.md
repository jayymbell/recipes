# Chicken Stock

**Time Estimate:** Prep: 15 min | Simmer: 3–4 hrs | Cool & Strain: 30 min  
**Servings:** 6–8 cups

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 whole Chicken (3–4 lbs), or 2–3 lbs Chicken Bones  
- [ ] 2 Carrots, roughly chopped  
- [ ] 2 Celery stalks, roughly chopped  
- [ ] 1 Onion, quartered  
- [ ] 4 cloves Garlic, smashed  
- [ ] 2 Bay Leaves  
- [ ] 1 tsp Whole Black Peppercorns  
- [ ] 1 tsp Salt (adjust to taste)  
- [ ] Optional: fresh herbs (thyme, parsley, rosemary)  

## Instructions

- Place chicken or bones in a large stockpot  
- Add carrots, celery, onion, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns, and herbs  
- Fill pot with cold water to cover ingredients (about 10 cups)  
- Bring to a gentle boil over medium-high heat  
- Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered 3–4 hours, skimming foam occasionally  
- Remove from heat and strain stock through a fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth into a large bowl  
- Discard solids  
- Let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate or freeze in portions  

## Notes

- For a richer flavor, roast the chicken/bones at 400°F (204°C) for 30–40 minutes before simmering  
- Refrigerated stock will keep 3–4 days; frozen stock lasts up to 3 months  
- Avoid over-salting; season later when using the stock in recipes  
- Use for soups, risottos, sauces, or braises

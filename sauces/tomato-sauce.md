# Tomato Sauce

Servings: 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] 1 small Onion, finely diced  
- [ ] 2–3 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 1 can (28 oz) Crushed Tomatoes or Whole Peeled Tomatoes, blended  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Oregano  
- [ ] 1 tsp Dried Basil  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Salt (adjust to taste)  
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Black Pepper  
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Sugar (optional, to balance acidity)  
- [ ] Optional: 1/4 tsp Red Pepper Flakes for heat  
- [ ] Optional: Fresh Basil or Parsley for garnish  

## Instructions

- Heat olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat  
- Add diced onion and sauté 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent  
- Add garlic and sauté 30 seconds until fragrant  
- Stir in crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, pepper, and sugar (if using)  
- Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally  
- Optional: Blend sauce with an immersion blender for smoother texture  
- Taste and adjust seasoning  
- Serve over pasta, pizza, or store in fridge for up to 5 days  

## Notes

- Can use fresh tomatoes (about 2 lbs) — blanch, peel, and crush before cooking  
- Simmer longer for richer, more concentrated flavor  
- Freezes well for up to 3 months

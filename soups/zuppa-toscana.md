# Zuppa Toscana

Servings: 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb Italian Sausage, sliced or crumbled  
- [ ] 4–5 Medium Potatoes, thinly sliced or diced  
- [ ] 1 medium Onion, diced  
- [ ] 2–3 cloves Garlic, minced  
- [ ] 4 cups Chicken Broth  
- [ ] 1 cup Heavy Cream  
- [ ] 2 cups Kale, chopped  
- [ ] 2 tbsp Olive Oil  
- [ ] Salt & Black Pepper, to taste  
- [ ] Red Pepper Flakes (optional)  

## Instructions

- Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat  
- Add sausage and cook until browned; remove and set aside  
- Add onion and garlic to the pot; sauté 3–4 minutes until softened  
- Add potatoes and chicken broth; bring to a boil, then simmer 10–15 minutes until potatoes are tender  
- Return sausage to the pot  
- Stir in kale and cook 2–3 minutes until wilted  
- Reduce heat and stir in heavy cream  
- Season with salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if desired  
- Serve hot with crusty bread  

## Notes

- Use spicy or mild Italian sausage depending on preference  
- For a lower-fat version, use half-and-half instead of heavy cream  
- Can also add bacon for extra flavor  
- Leftovers taste even better the next day
